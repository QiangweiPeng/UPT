Highlights

From perturbation dynamics to in silico design.

U-Pert connects forward response prediction, mechanistic interpretation, and target-driven perturbation design within a unified unbalanced dynamics framework.

01

Unbalanced perturbation dynamics

Joint modeling of perturbation-induced cell-state transitions and birth-death dynamics.

U-Pert learns both transcriptomic state changes and cell proliferation/apoptosis from destructive, unpaired single-cell perturbation snapshots.

02

Forward evolution

Generalization to unseen perturbations and contexts.

Forward simulation predicts unobserved cellular responses while supporting condition- and context-specific mechanism discovery from learned velocity and growth fields.

03

Inverse design

Target-driven search over genetic edits and drug perturbations.

Given desired post-perturbation outcomes, such as target DEG programs or cell-type compositions, U-Pert can screen effective genetic edits or drug perturbations in silico.
